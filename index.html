The Standard

Does buying a cap or patch get my hero into the archive?
No. Purchases support the archive. They don't buy placement in it. Anyone may nominate a hero — free, always — and every nomination goes through the same standards, blind to money.

Will every nomination be published?
Every nomination is reviewed. Not every nomination is published. The archive honors Veterans, First Responders, Community Leaders, and Historic Places whose service can be verified — that standard is what makes the honor mean something.

Are honorees ever charged?
Never. No fee to nominate, no fee to be honored, no fee to stay. Published honorees are never altered and never removed.

Is this a government archive?
No. The Best of America is a private archive, founded 2003, with no government affiliation.

Materials & Care

Cap: 100% cotton brushed twill. Patch: embroidered twill with a black bound edge. Spot clean or wash cold, hang dry. Do not iron directly on the embroidery — steam from the reverse.
