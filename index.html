A Permanent National Archive

The Best of America™ National Archive is an independent, permanent archive. We're not a government agency or a museum gift shop. We preserve the lives of veterans, first responders, Gold Star families, pioneers and builders whose lives are the best of America. Each record is verified, sourced and kept for good.

Nominations and individual recognitions are always free at TheBestofAmerica.org.

From America's Aerospace Valley

We're based in Lancaster, California, in the Antelope Valley. The B-2 stealth bomber was assembled at Air Force Plant 42 in Palmdale, and the B-21 Raider is being built there now. Just up the road is Edwards Air Force Base, where Chuck Yeager broke the sound barrier in 1947 and the Space Shuttle landed.

The base carries the name of Capt. Glen W. Edwards, the young test pilot who gave his life in 1948. He is preserved in The Best of America™ archive. When you wear the seal, you wear that lineage.

Why We Make Apparel
